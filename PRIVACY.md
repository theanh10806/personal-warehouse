# Privacy Policy — Shin Warehouse Management

Last updated: November 19, 2026

## 1. Overview

Shin Warehouse Management ("the App") is a desktop application for personal
inventory management. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses,
how it is stored, and your rights as a user.

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal information.
Specifically, the App does NOT:

- Collect your name, email, phone number, or any account information
- Transmit any data to our servers or any third party
- Include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- Include advertising or third-party SDKs
- Access your contacts, calendar, photos, or files outside its own folder

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you enter into the App is stored **only on your device** in the folder:

    Documents\MyWarehouse

This includes:
- Inventory items (name, quantity, location, notes, photos you attach)
- Movement history (stock-in, stock-out, adjustments)
- App settings and preferences
- Local SQLite database files

This data never leaves your device. We have no access to it.

## 4. Camera Permission (Barcode Scanning)

If you use the barcode scanning feature, the App accesses your device camera
**only while you are actively scanning**. Camera frames are processed in
real-time on your device to decode barcodes. No images or video are stored
or transmitted.

You can deny camera permission at any time via Windows Settings → Privacy
→ Camera. The App remains fully functional without it (manual input still works).

## 5. Internet Access

The App works entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection
to perform its core functions. If internet access is used (e.g., to check
for updates), no personal data is transmitted.

## 6. Data Backup and Export

The App provides features to export data (CSV) or back up your database.
These operations save files to locations **you choose** on your device.
We do not receive these files.

## 7. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the App does NOT delete your data in Documents\MyWarehouse.
Your data remains on your device so you can install a newer version later
without data loss. You may delete the folder manually at any time.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13. Since no data is collected, the App is safe for all ages.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top.
Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
